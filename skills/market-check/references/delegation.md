# Set up and review the research worker

Read this as the coordinator of market-check. An assigned worker follows the skill's research method directly and does not delegate.

## Select one inexpensive worker

Read `python3 scripts/portfolio.py settings` for the configured model and reasoning effort. Select them through the host's actual delegation tool. With a null model, choose an available inexpensive worker only when the host provides enough information to identify one. Record the real selection; model names in prompts or frontmatter do not select a model.

If the configured model, browsing or model-selection tool is unavailable, report the scan pending. Keep the saved idea and avoid silently choosing a more expensive fallback or doing the research on the coordinator. An explicitly authorized alternative takes precedence.

Give the worker a fresh, minimal context: this skill's absolute path, resolved idea folder, market constraints, and ownership of only its MARKET.md. State that it is not alone in the workspace and must preserve others' edits. The worker must not delegate again or capture ideas. It follows the skill's six-query/eight-page budget and stops. The coordinator can continue independent work meanwhile.

## Review the result

Read the report and reconcile each feature claim with the source log. A page that failed to open cannot support a documented claim; snippets remain unverified leads and cannot establish a gap.

Reopen at most two sources when a material claim is questionable, recording any extra review reads. Prefer marking uncertainty over repeating the scan. Preserve human annotations and append corrections with their reason. Report the result and link the file. Further research or downstream procedures require an applicable user request.
