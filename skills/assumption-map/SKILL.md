# Map the assumptions that matter

Read [the shared contract](../../docs/CONTRACT.md). Trigger on a request to map an idea's decisive unknowns.

## Inputs and ownership

Require CONTEXT.md. Read available OFFER.md, MARKET.md and CHALLENGE.md when present, plus relevant EVIDENCE.md and existing ASSUMPTIONS.md. Own only ASSUMPTIONS.md. Missing market/challenge inputs allow a provisional map; list missing inputs without calling their skills. Append a dated revision and preserve annotations, earlier predictions and stable assumption IDs.

## Procedure and output

Identify approximately 3–5 assumptions maximum in the current map; fewer is better when sufficient. Select claims whose falsity would change whether to invest, not routine engineering implementation risks. Cover actual pain and reachable users before technical feasibility unless feasibility is genuinely the bottleneck. For commercial ideas, consider buying authority, a reachable acquisition route, willingness to pay for the scoped outcome, and delivery/support economics. Select only decisive unknowns within the existing 3–5 limit. A service, productized service, or software product may need different evidence; conceptual notes need no invented buyer.

For each active assumption (A1, A2, etc.) record:
- A specific falsifiable claim and why the idea depends on it.
- Current evidence with source/date, including contradictory observations.
- Uncertainty (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) and consequence if false.
- Importance (DECISIVE / MATERIAL); no decimal scores or giant weighted formula.
- Cheapest credible test and what observation could refute it.

Order by importance and uncertainty. Name the single assumption to test first and why learning about it outranks the others. Do not multiply similar assumptions; merge them while retaining their original IDs as superseded. Assumptions proven false do not disappear from history. A later revision may replace the current set, but cannot silently move goalposts.

## Non-goals and handoff

No risk register, research run, state mutation, roadmap or full experiment program. Speculation is not an observation. Link validation-designer as an optional next procedure, never invoke it automatically.
