# Learn from what happened

Explain what the outcome teaches us about the original expectations.

Follow the [shared rules](../../docs/CONTRACT.md). For one idea, read CONTEXT.md and available historical OFFER, ASSUMPTIONS, VALIDATION predictions, EVIDENCE, STATE/GATE and RETROSPECTIVE notes. For a portfolio lesson, read the existing retrospectives.

## Review one idea

1. Recover the original thesis and predictions made **before** the outcome. Link to the dated originals. Missing expectations stay “not recorded”; do not reconstruct earlier confidence.
2. Describe actual observations, chronology and limitations, including contradictory evidence. Separate the user's explanation from your inference.
3. Compare expected demand, difficulty and outcome with what happened. For commercial work, include acquisition effort, customer value, collected revenue, delivery/support time and costs, repeat purchases/use and reasons for leaving where known. Distinguish gross revenue from profit and estimates from measured attribution.
4. Explain why the idea stopped, succeeded or changed direction, and the opportunity cost. Keep an unresolved lifecycle disposition visible without changing it.
5. End with one practical lesson and a falsifiable adjustment to the next decision.

Append to the idea's **RETROSPECTIVE.md** using `append-report idea-retrospective --idea <slug>`. If nothing has happened, state that outcome comparison is not yet possible and suggest recording a prediction in the next validation plan.

## Compare across ideas

When requested, append to workspace **CALIBRATION.md** using the same command without `--idea`. Give the sample count, comparable cases, counterexamples and uncertainty. Unrecorded abandoned ideas are missing from the sample.

Investigate possible patterns such as underestimated distribution/support effort or overvalued technical differentiation; do not assume them. One or two examples support tentative questions, not broad rules. Prefer one actionable calibration lesson. It becomes a global rule only if the user chooses it.
