# Design a testable offer

Turn an existing commercial idea into one specific offer a buyer could consider.

Follow the [shared rules](../../docs/CONTRACT.md). Read CONTEXT.md and available MARKET, CHALLENGE, ASSUMPTIONS, EVIDENCE and previous OFFER notes. If the idea has no commercial intent, say so and stop.

## Shape one offer

1. Identify the user, economic buyer and buying authority. Describe the painful job, current workaround and observed baseline; mark unknowns.
2. Propose a measurable outcome and time window. Explain how improvement could be attributed to the offer. Growth, time, cost, quality and risk reduction are all possible sources of value.
3. Choose a delivery format: service, productized service or software. Define the smallest useful pilot, scope exclusions, acceptance criteria, and effort/cost cap. New implementation still requires the BUILDING gate.
4. State a price hypothesis. Estimate acquisition, delivery and support effort plus direct costs. Separate revenue from profit and hypotheses from observed returns.
5. Name the actual proof available and its limits. A demo or another company's success is not evidence of this customer's outcome; leave guarantees out of an untested offer.
6. Make the next buying decision clear: one plausible acquisition route, onboarding access/data, customer responsibilities, handover and support. Reduce unnecessary friction without hiding obligations.
7. Identify the most consequential uncertainty and suggest one test, linking an existing assumption or experiment when available.

## Save the offer

Append to **OFFER.md** using `append-report offer-design`, with stable versions O1, O2, etc. The [offer template](../../templates/OFFER.md) is optional. Label statements as observed, sourced, estimated or untested.

An offer can be drafted before validation; it never advances lifecycle state. Finish with the offer and its key uncertainty. Leave the experiment to validation-designer and actual observations to EVIDENCE.md's documented owner.
